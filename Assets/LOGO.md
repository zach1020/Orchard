## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: production macOS app icon and brand mark for an original local-AI app called Orchard. Create ONE polished square app icon, centered, straight-on, orthographic, with true transparent alpha outside the rounded-square icon tile. 1024 x 1024 composition. The tile is a very dark obsidian / OLED black rounded square with subtle liquid-glass thickness and extremely understated edge reflections. It occupies about 88 percent of the canvas, with even transparent margins; no cast shadow outside the tile. On it, a bold original abstract orchard-leaf emblem: a single clean luminous cyan folded leaf whose curved stem and negative space suggest an open letter O, with one short electric-magenta accent along the lower-right inner edge. The leaf should be simple, confident, organic yet geometric, a unique logo silhouette readable at 16 to 32 pixels. Primary cyan #40F0F0, accent magenta #FF47A8. Elegant cyberpunk, precise and restrained. The mark occupies about 60 percent of the tile. Subtle glass material within the emblem, crisp high-contrast edges, very small soft cyan glow; black interior remains black. No letters, no wordmark, no text, no grid, no mockup, no screenshot, no circuits or tiny details, no starbursts, no Apple logo, no multiple alternatives, no border frame around the entire canvas. Deliver a single final icon asset.

The generator returned a 1254 × 1254 PNG. The ICNS contains the standard 16, 32, 128, 256, and 512 point sizes at 1x and 2x.
